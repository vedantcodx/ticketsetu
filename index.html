How TicketSetu Works

Connecting train travelers across India in 3 simple steps. No confusing bids, no hefty platform fees.

01

1. Search & Discover

Enter your origin, destination, and travel date. Filter by coach class like Sleeper, 3AC, 2AC or price to spot matching confirmed tickets.

02

2. Contact Seller

Get in touch directly with the passenger via direct phone call or WhatsApp. Review berth details, timing, and coordinate the exchange smoothly.

03

3. Travel Hassle-Free

Settle the fair face value directly with the passenger, receive the ticket details, and enjoy your comfortable train journey without waiting lists.
