About TicketSetu

Bridging the gap between travelers with unused seats and commuters in need of confirmed train journeys across India.

Our Story & Mission

The "Setu" (Bridge) for Indian Railways

Every day on the Indian Railway network, thousands of passengers cancel confirmed tickets at steep penalty deductions, while millions of passengers are stranded on high waiting lists (WL/RAC). TicketSetu was created to bridge this disparity.

100% Peer-to-Peer

Direct communication between actual travelers. No agency cut, no commission fees, and no artificial price inflation.

Zero Seat Wastage

Every confirmed seat not boarded represents an empty berth and a missed journey. TicketSetu ensures high capacity utilization.

Community Trust

Transparent communication, verified phone contacts, and clear guidelines encourage mutual respect and fair dealing.

Guidelines for Safe Ticket Exchange

Please follow these recommended best practices to ensure a smooth, legitimate, and secure ticket exchange.

Verify Ticket & Passenger Details

Always inspect the physical ticket printout or IRCTC digital m-ticket SMS/PDF to confirm train number, boarding date, and class.

Exchange at Printed Face Value

Never pay more than the printed fare. TicketSetu actively discourages commercial scalping or inflated rates.

Direct Phone / WhatsApp Contact

Speak directly over the phone to confirm passenger details, boarding station timing, and exchange logistics.

Indian Railways Compliance

Per Indian Railways regulations, tickets can be officially transferred to blood relatives (parents, spouse, children, siblings) at station counters up to 24 hours prior to departure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

What is TicketSetu and how does it work?
TicketSetu is a free peer-to-peer bulletin board. Passengers with confirmed train tickets who cannot travel post their ticket details and contact info. Travelers searching for seats on those dates can contact the seller directly via call or WhatsApp to arrange the exchange.
Does TicketSetu charge any commission or booking fee?
No. TicketSetu is completely free to use with zero commission. We do not process payments between buyers and sellers; you coordinate directly at the original printed ticket value.
Can Indian Railway tickets be officially transferred?
Yes. According to Indian Railways (IRCTC) rules, a confirmed ticket can be officially transferred to blood relations (mother, father, brother, sister, son, daughter, or spouse) by submitting an application along with relationship proof at a railway reservation counter at least 24 hours prior to scheduled train departure.
How do I remove my ticket once someone takes it?
Simply navigate to the "Add Ticket" page and scroll down to the "My Listed Tickets" section. Click the "Delete" button next to your ticket to immediately remove it from all public search results.
How can I protect myself from fraudulent listings?
Always request a copy of the official IRCTC booking confirmation SMS or PDF, verify that the train time and date align with your plans, pay only through secure UPI or in person at the station, and never click any unknown payment links or share OTPs.
