Tips for Sellers

Fair Original Pricing

Keep ticket price close to or lower than the original fare printed on your IRCTC ticket for quick pickup.

Protect Your Privacy

Never share credit card, OTPs, or full banking passwords. Ticket exchanges require only phone coordination.

Delete Once Sold

Once you find a buyer, delete your listing from the "My Listed Tickets" section below to stop receiving calls.

Fair Exchange Ethos

TicketSetu is built on community goodwill to prevent seat wastage and help passengers with urgent travel needs. Commercial ticket scalping or black marketing is strictly prohibited.
